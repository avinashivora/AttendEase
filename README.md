# AttendEase

> **A live attendance management system designed to simplify attendance collection,
> validation, and reporting for students and faculty.**

## Overview

AttendEase is an attendance management platform built to streamline the
collection, processing, and reporting of attendance data across academic
classes.

The system was deployed and tested in a live academic environment, supporting
**3,000+ users across 100+ classes**.

Rather than treating attendance as a simple record-keeping task, AttendEase
automates the workflow from attendance collection and validation to
defaulter identification and reporting.

## Key Highlights

- 👥 **3,000+ users** supported in a live environment
- 🏫 **100+ classes** managed through the platform
- 📍 **Geofenced attendance verification** for location-aware attendance marking
- 🔄 Automated attendance processing and validation
- 📊 Automated defaulter identification and reporting
- ⚡ Reduced report generation from approximately **1 week to under 5 minutes**
- ⏱️ Saved approximately **60+ faculty hours per month**
- 🔐 Role-based workflows for different system users

## How It Works

The general attendance workflow consists of:

1. **Attendance Collection**  
   Attendance records are submitted through the platform.

2. **Validation**  
   Submitted records are checked against relevant attendance and user data.

3. **Processing**  
   Validated attendance records are processed and associated with the
   corresponding classes and students.

4. **Defaulter Identification**  
   Attendance data is evaluated against the applicable requirements to identify
   students requiring attention.

5. **Reporting**  
   Processed data is transformed into reports for faculty and administrative
   use.

This workflow replaces a largely manual process with a centralized,
repeatable data-processing pipeline.

## Geolocation & Attendance Verification

AttendEase incorporates **geofencing** into the attendance workflow to help
verify that attendance is being recorded from an expected location.

The location-aware component adds an additional validation layer to the
attendance process rather than relying solely on manually submitted records.

## Impact

AttendEase was used in a live academic environment with **3,000+ users and
100+ classes**.

The automated reporting workflow reduced the time required to generate
defaulter reports from approximately **one week to under five minutes**,
while reducing the associated faculty workload by approximately
**60+ hours per month**.

These improvements came primarily from centralizing attendance data and
automating the processing and reporting workflow.
